Checkout aur payment page: prepaid order ke liye bana design

4 screen hain. Safed gol number niche ki list se milte hain. Rakam store ke asli niyam se hai (coupon, 15% online, COD ₹99).

Is design me kya-kya hai (number screen ke gol number se milte hain)

  1. 1Sirf 2 step, upar bachat hamesha saamne. "You save ₹849" patti har step pe dikhti hai, taaki customer ko yaad rahe wo kya kho dega.
  2. 2Phone number sabse pehle. Number likhte hi wo save ho jata hai; customer chhod bhi de to aapke paas uska number aur bag rehta hai (yaad-dihani ke liye).
  3. 3PIN daalte hi city aur state apne aap. Purane customer ko "Use saved address" ek tap me. Kam likhna = kam chhodna.
  4. 4Sabse accha coupon apne aap lagta hai. Customer ko code dhoondhna nahi padta; hatana ho to hata sakta hai.
  5. 5Online payment bada aur pehle se chuna hua. Sunehra card, bada daam, "Best price" aur 4 asli fayde.
  6. 6UPI app ke seedhe button. Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm / koi bhi UPI. Dabate hi Razorpay usi app ke saath khulta hai, ek step kam.
  7. 7COD chhota aur niche, poori sachchai ke saath. "₹214 zyada lagega" saaf likha hai. Chhupaya nahi, bas kam ubhara hai.
  8. 8Dono ka seedha hisaab ek patti me. Online ₹650 banam COD ₹864: farq aankhon ke saamne.
  9. 9COD chunne pe ek baar rok-ke poochhna. "Online pe ₹214 bachenge, pakka COD?" Ek hi baar, zabardasti nahi; "COD hi rakho" ka button bhi hai.
  10. 10Payment fail ho to order nahi jata. Wahi order, "Dobara try karo" ya "Doosra UPI app", aur WhatsApp madad. Paisa kata ho to order number ke saath seedha message.
  11. 11Bharosa pay button ke theek paas. "Razorpay secured · UPI PIN hum nahi dekhte · 7-day returns". Jahan dar lagta hai wahin jawab.
  12. 12Asli offer ki asli ghadi. NAVRATRI coupon 20 Oct ko khatam hota hai; wahi ghadi yahan. Nakli "5 minute me khatam" nahi.

Sachchi baat: "99% prepaid" ka vaada koi design nahi kar sakta, kyunki kuch customer sirf COD hi lete hain. Ye design prepaid ko sabse aasaan aur sabse sasta raasta banata hai aur COD ko mehnga aur ek kadam door rakhta hai. Aur sakht karna ho to do switch aap chala sakte ho: (a) COD ka advance ₹99 se badhana, (b) kisi rakam ke upar COD band karna. Ye sab bata do to main asli checkout me bana dunga. Pasand aaye to "laga do" likh do; kuch hatana / jodna ho to number bata do.
