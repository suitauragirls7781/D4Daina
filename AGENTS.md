# Suit Aura Girls - Brand & UI/UX Guidelines

These rules are set for the "Suit Aura Girls" women's fashion storefront. All code, styles, and UI adjustments must adhere to these guidelines. (Updated 2026-10-09: the owner chose **"Emerald Heritage"** (look "B" of three premium previews) for every page at every width, admin included, and asked for every section to be redesigned to it. It replaces "Midnight glass" (dark, gold, frosted panels) and every earlier look: do not bring back dark pages, gold fills, glass blur, pills or floating bars. The brand is always "Suit Aura Girls".)

---

## 🎨 1. Core Color System (Emerald Heritage)
Ivory pages, white panels with a thin warm line, deep emerald for the header, menu, footer, buttons and links, a little champagne as thin rules and on emerald, and ruby only for "% off" and the saved heart. Use the token classes and the helpers, never raw hex in components. Tokens are defined once in `src/index.css` (`@theme`, mirrored on `:root` as `--sag-*`); the shared shapes are in the "Emerald Heritage" block near the end of that file. Token and helper NAMES are kept from the earlier systems (`lilac`, `peach`, `mint`, `sky`, `butter`, `sun`, `sag-glass`, `--sag-on-gold` are only names): do not read a colour or a material into a name.

- **Page** — `page` ivory `#F8F4EC`; `surface` white; `sunken` a warm tint `#F1ECE0` (a notice, the "In short" box, the review band, photo placeholders).
- **Text** — `ink` `#1B1B19`, `ink-muted` `#5E5B55`, `ink-faint` `#77736B`.
- **Emerald** `#0F3D33` (darker `#0A2C25`) — `brand` / `sale` / `ok` / `dark`: the header, the phone menu, the footer, the editorial band, the primary button, links, eyebrows, selected states, ticks, progress. Text on an emerald fill is ivory: `text-[color:var(--sag-on-gold)]`, or put `sag-on-emerald` on the surface (it turns text ivory, muted text soft green, eyebrows and links champagne and lines champagne-tinted).
- **Champagne** `#CDB58A` — `highlight` and `var(--sag-champagne)`: thin rules, the ornament, the arch line, labels on emerald. Never a filled champagne button or block. `star` / `sun` `#A8894F` for review stars.
- **Ruby** `#9B2C3B` — `accent`: "% off", price reductions in a price list, the filled wishlist heart. Nothing else.
- **Lines** — `line` `#E5DDCC`; `control` `#B4AC9C` (field and chip edges).
- **Outside these, only where meaning needs it**: `danger` `#B3261E` for errors (with an icon and words), the green inside the WhatsApp glyph, and the product photos.
- **Negative constraint**: no dark pages, no gold fills, no `bg-white` / `text-white` / `text-black` / raw `rgba()` in components, no blur, no glows, no gradients; shadows only as the soft one under a bar that stays on screen or a dialog.
- **Print** (the invoice, the packing slip) stays black on white.

Helper classes live in `src/index.css`: `sag-glass` (a white panel with the line, 10px corners), `sag-glass-strong` (a warm tinted panel), `sag-glass-bar` (a white bar that stays on screen), `sag-btn-primary` (emerald, ivory small capitals), `sag-btn-outline` (an emerald outline that fills on hover), `sag-btn-light` (the ivory button on an emerald band), `sag-link`, `sag-lnk` (a small-capitals link with a thin line, for "View all"), `sag-input`, `sag-label`, `sag-eyebrow`, `sag-chip` (white box; selected = emerald with ivory text), `sag-badge`, `sag-card`, `sag-row`, `sag-bar`, `sag-num`, `sag-scroll-x`, `sag-on-emerald`, `sag-orn` (the ornament: two champagne rules and a diamond), `sag-arch` (arch-topped photo frame), `sag-marquee`. Page and section titles go through `src/components/SectionTitle.tsx` (eyebrow, serif title, ornament; centred). The four trust facts are worded once in `src/components/Assurance.tsx`; reuse that component instead of writing new claims.

## 💎 2. Visual Language
**Read this first**: the look of every part is the Emerald Heritage look described here and in section 1. Several bullets further down were written for earlier looks and are kept for what each part must CONTAIN and how it behaves; wherever one of them describes a shape or colour differently ("glass", "pill", "gold", "floating", "hairline box", "black button", the hero "collage"), this paragraph and the bullets marked (Emerald Heritage) win.

- **(Emerald Heritage) Shapes**: panels and boxes 10px corners with the 1px warm line and no shadow; photos 8px corners (category photos are arch-topped with a thin champagne arch line outside them); buttons, fields and chips 8px corners and 44-56px high; real circles only for icon buttons, swatches, avatars, count badges, radio dots and timeline dots.
- **(Emerald Heritage) Typography**: **Cormorant Garamond** (`font-display`, weight 600, set large: 30-40px titles on phones, up to 54-60px on desktop) for the wordmark, page and section titles, the hero title, dialog titles, the product name on the product page, totals' labels and a few figures (the deal percent, countdown digits, a coupon code). **Figtree** for everything else. Eyebrows, buttons, small links, field labels and tabs are small letter-spaced capitals; everything else is sentence case. Prices use tabular figures (`sag-num`). Body >= 13px, labels >= 10.5px, text in fields 16px.
- **(Emerald Heritage) Section pattern**: a centred `SectionTitle` (a short true eyebrow, the serif title, the ornament), the content, then "View all" as a centred `sag-lnk` under it. One section of the homepage is the emerald editorial band (the featured collection: two arch photos, the second set lower, a large serif title, a true line and the ivory button).
- **(Emerald Heritage) Header**: above it a dark-emerald strip with the store's real messages moving slowly in champagne capitals (it scrolls away with the page; hidden on checkout; still with reduced motion). The header itself is one emerald bar that stays on top and NEVER changes height while scrolling: on phones and tablets the menu and search buttons at the left, the serif wordmark "SUIT AURA GIRLS" with the ornament in the centre, wishlist and bag with real counts at the right; on desktop the search field, the mark and wordmark, the account buttons, and under a thin champagne line the menu row in small capitals. The search panel opens over the page.
- **(Emerald Heritage) Phone menu**: a full-height emerald panel from the left: the links as large serif rows with thin champagne lines and real counts, in folding groups, the shopper's row at the bottom. **Bottom navigation (phones)** stays (the owner's "rising bump"): a white drawn bar with the line, the current tab in an emerald bubble.
- **(Emerald Heritage) Hero**: per slide the real photo at 4:5, full width, slides cross-fade and the shown photo zooms very slowly; under it the "01 / 05" count with thin bars (the current one filling over 6 seconds), an eyebrow, the big serif title, the real price with "% off" in ruby, the emerald "Shop now" and "Explore" as a small-capitals link. Desktop: photo left, words right. Nothing is written on a photo.
- **(Emerald Heritage) Product card**: no box: the 4:5 photo with 8px corners and the heart in a small white circle, a tiny muted capitals label (real badge or category), the name in two lines, the rating line when real, the price row ("% off" in ruby), and "Add to bag" as a quiet row with a thin top line and a plus.
- **(Emerald Heritage) Footer**: an emerald band: the centred wordmark with the ornament, round champagne-outlined social buttons, link columns with champagne capitals headings, the address and the small print.
- **(Emerald Heritage) Shop by category is alive** (owner, 2026-10-09, `ShopByCategory.tsx`, "Shop by category, alive" in `src/index.css`): the arches rise in one after another and their photos unveil; a short emerald stroke runs without end along each arch's champagne border; one category at a time is "in the light" for 2.8 seconds (it lifts, a second champagne line opens, its photo cross-fades to the next REAL photo of that category, its name turns emerald and a thin line fills under it), and on a phone the row follows the light until the shopper touches the row. The real count of designs is shown under each name. It pauses off screen and while the pointer is on a category; reduced motion shows it still.
- **(Emerald Heritage) Motion**: calm and one-time: pages and sections rise in, ornaments and thin lines draw, photos zoom slowly under the pointer, buttons fill from the left, steps slide in, counts settle, the order timeline fills down to the current stage. The only thing that moves by itself is the announcement strip and the hero. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes all motion.
- **Soft and round**: panels 22-28px radius, photos 14-22px, buttons and chips are pills, icon buttons are round glass circles. Sections sit on the backdrop with a serif title; featured pieces (festive offer, hero, deal, review, facts, product cards, forms, summaries) are glass panels.
- **Typography**: two families only. **Figtree** for all text and every number, at regular and medium weights (semibold for prices, totals and buttons). **Fraunces** (`font-display`, weight 500) for the wordmark, page titles, section titles, the hero title and dialog titles; never for prices, product names, order numbers, forms, buttons or navigation. Eyebrows and a few small labels are letter-spaced capitals in gold; everything else is sentence case. Prices use tabular figures (`sag-num`). Body ≥ 13px, labels ≥ 10.5px, text in fields 16px.
- **Buttons**: primary is a gold pill with dark text ("Buy now", "Checkout", pay, "Shop now"); secondary is a glass pill with light text. Links are gold text. Tap targets at least 44×44px from tablet width up (36-40px on phones, see Phone scale).
- **Icons**: thin outline icons in the text colour or gold; icon buttons are round glass circles.
- **Floating bars**: on phones the header pill, the bottom navigation (current tab = gold pill with dark text) and the product page's buy bar float with a small margin from the screen edges. At most one fixed bottom bar per screen.
- **Motion** (owner, 2026-10-08: calm, one-time, premium, on every page): the new page rises in; every section rises in once as it comes into view (`src/utils/reveal.ts`), with a thin champagne rule drawing under its title; buttons fill from the left on hover and press slightly; product photos zoom slowly under the pointer; the heart beats once, the bag icon bumps once, changed totals settle in. The hero photo drifts slowly, its words rise in, slides change by themselves every 6 seconds with a thin progress line. The festive panel carries a live countdown to the coupon's real last day and a slowly drifting real photo; the deal's price counts once to the real price and shows a clock only for a real end time. No spinning rings, bouncing stickers, waving letters, blinking or marquees. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes all motion.
- **Header** (owner's pick 2-3, 2026-10-08): one white sticky bar: the round logo mark and the wordmark "SUIT AURA GIRLS" (Fraunces capitals) with the small line "Ethnic wear · Delivery across India" at the left, wishlist and bag (real counts) at the right. Under it, on phones and tablets: a square menu button and the always-open search field, then the announcement as a tinted line (`bg-sunken`, champagne text) that rotates the real messages (the live festive round, the online payment percent, cash on delivery when on). The search row and the announcement are part of the page, not of the fixed header (owner, 2026-10-09: the sliding header made the page jump): they scroll away with the page and never come back mid-page; once they are out of sight the header pill shows a menu and a search button instead (on narrow phones in place of the heart). The fixed header must never change height while scrolling, and the search panel opens over the page without pushing it. Desktop: the mark and wordmark, a square hairline search field and the menu row in small capitals.
- **Search**: an underlined field; with an empty field "Trending now" (real product photos with prices) and "Collections" as plain rows with real counts.
- **Phone menu** (owner's pick 2 of the "sleek sidebar" previews, 2026-10-08): a floating dark glass sidebar with a margin from the screen edges: the logo mark, the name and close; a search pill; then groups that fold (Shop, Collections, Your account, Help), each line an icon, its name and a real count in a small badge; the page the shopper is on is a gold pill with dark text; at the bottom the shopper's row (Sign in, or their name and "My account") (`MobileMenuDrawer.tsx`).
- **Bottom navigation (phones)** (owner's pick 2 of the "morphing blob" previews, 2026-10-08, "rising bump"): a floating dark frosted bar a little above the bottom edge whose outline is one drawn shape: it swells up around the current tab and a gold bubble with that tab's icon sits in the swell; on a tab change the swell and the bubble glide across together (`MobileAppNavbar.tsx`, `sag-blobnav*` in `src/index.css`). Home, Shop, Wishlist, Bag (real count), Account, names always shown, the current one in gold. On a page that is none of the five the bar is flat. At most one fixed bottom bar per screen.
- **Hero** (owner's pick 4-2, 2026-10-08, "magazine"): per slide a collage: a narrow vertical caption in small champagne capitals (the owner's badge text, else "New season"), the main photo large and a second real photo of the same style (else the next slide's photo) overlapping its lower right corner in a white frame; under it, left-aligned, the serif title, the real price and "% off", a black "Shop now" and an underlined "Explore". Slides change by themselves every 6 seconds with a thin progress line and "01 / 05". Nothing is written on a photo. Desktop: collage left, words right.
- **Trust facts**: a plain grid of thin champagne icon + the fact between hairlines (two columns on phones, four on desktop). "How your order works" is a hairline box with three numbered rows.

The photo (4:5, square) with the heart in a small white circle; under it a real badge as a tiny champagne label, the name in two lines, a small rating line with one champagne star when real, the price line, and "Add to bag" as a quiet underlined control. ## 📸 3. Model & Media Treatment
- **Clear catalog images**: never cover product photos with gradients, text or badges. The one thing allowed on a product card photo is the wishlist heart in a white circle in the top-right corner (the brand watermark sits top-left). Badges and prices sit below the image.
- **No watermark overlaps**: catalog photos already carry the brand watermark; never overlay logos on them.
- Portrait photos use `aspect-[4/5]` with `object-cover object-top`.
- `public/cropped_circle_image.png` is another store's logo and must never be shown; the round mark in the header is `public/suit-aura-mark-132.webp`, cut from this store's own logo.

- **Where photos and reels live** (owner, 2026-10-07: moved off Cloudinary, which is gone from this project and must not come back): the Cloudflare R2 bucket `suitauragirls-media`, read straight from `https://media.suitauragirls.in` (Cloudflare's CDN; a photo costs no Worker request and no bandwidth fee). Nothing is resized on the fly. Every photo is stored in ready-made files: `i/<folder>/<id>/w160|w320|w480|w640|w828|w1100|w1600.webp` plus `j480.jpg` (emails) and `j1200.jpg` (link previews, the AI photo reader); a reel is `v/<folder>/<id>/play.mp4` and `poster.jpg`. The address saved in the database is `…/w1100.webp` or `…/play.mp4`.
  - Show a photo only through `getCleanImageUrl(url, width)` (`src/utils/imageHelper.ts`), which picks the stored size that fits; never write a size into a URL by hand. Links saved before the move are translated by `toMediaUrl` in `src/lib/media.ts`.
  - Uploads: the browser makes every size first (`makeImageFiles` in `src/lib/media.ts`), then sends each file to `PUT /api/media` with a grant the server signed for that one photo (`createMediaGrant` in `api/_lib/media.js`; admin via `/api/admin-config?action=media-grant`, customers via the review and return forms). A 5 MB phone photo goes up as about 0.3-1 MB in all. Reel videos are stored as uploaded: MP4 only, 50 MB at most.
  - Deleting a photo deletes every stored size of it (`deleteMedia`).
  - Admin → Usage & limits shows the bucket's storage and requests against R2's free amounts (10 GB, 1 million writes and 10 million reads a month) and an estimate of how many visits the free plan can carry, from measured numbers only.

## 🧭 4b. Admin panel (redesigned 2026-10-08; Emerald Heritage colours 2026-10-09: an emerald sidebar, a white top bar, white panels)
The owner is not technical: every admin page uses plain words and the same skeleton (serif title with one line saying what the page is for, 2-4 real key numbers in small glass tiles, filters in one row, the main list or form in titled glass panels, one gold primary action, clear empty states). Shared pieces live in `src/components/admin/adminUi.tsx`, `kidsToysShared.tsx`, `PaymentBadge.tsx` and `AdminPaymentGateways.tsx`.
- **Dashboard**: "Right now" (people on the site this moment, visitors today, orders and sales today), "Today's to-do", cash to collect on cash-on-delivery orders, live visitors with their page trail, "What customers are doing", visitors by hour, Today / This week / This month, the path to an order (funnel), the last 30 days, "Needs your attention", top products, sources and devices. **Reports** is the day / week / month history with CSV.
- **Live visitors** come from the `presence` table (one row per visitor: city from Cloudflare, device, source, current page, the last 12 pages; never the IP address). The storefront sends a small signal on each page change and every 2 minutes (`src/utils/presence.ts`, `api/_lib/presence.js`, event `presence` on `/api/track`); the admin reads `GET /api/admin-config?action=live` every 15 seconds while the dashboard is open. Presence has its own daily write budget (`PRESENCE_WRITE_BUDGET`): when it is used up only the live view pauses for the day; visit counting and orders are never affected.
- Top bar: search everything (`/` or Ctrl+K), quick actions, an optional sound for new paid orders. Order details carry a printable packing slip and ready-made WhatsApp messages.
- Every number in the admin is real; a day without data says "No data".

## ✅ 5. Honesty Rules
- Never show fake coupons, fake ratings/reviews, invented delivery dates, countdowns that reset, stock numbers that are not real, or service claims the owner has not confirmed.
- Every price, discount, coupon and total shown in the browser is only a preview; the server (`api/orders.js`) recalculates everything before payment.
