# Suit Aura Girls - Brand & UI/UX Guidelines

These rules are set for the "Suit Aura Girls" women's fashion storefront. All code, styles, and UI adjustments must adhere to these guidelines. (Updated 2026-10-08, evening: after two glassmorphism previews the owner chose **"Midnight glass"** for every page at every width, admin included. It replaces "Noir & Champagne" (white, square, hairlines) of the same morning and every earlier look. The brand is always "Suit Aura Girls".)

---

## 🎨 1. Core Color System (Midnight glass)
A deep night backdrop with soft gold and wine light, frosted translucent panels with thin light edges, light text and gold buttons. Use the token classes and the glass helpers, never raw hex in components. Tokens are defined once in `src/index.css` (`@theme`, mirrored on `:root` as `--sag-*`); the shared shapes are in the "Midnight glass" block at the end of that file. The token NAMES are kept from the earlier systems (`lilac`, `peach`, `mint`, `sky`, `butter`, `sun`, `surface`, `sunken` are only names and are translucent tints now): do not read a colour into a name.

- **Backdrop** — `page` `#0F0D12`, with three soft lights (gold, wine, night blue) drawn once behind everything (`body::before`). Page wrappers and sections never get an opaque background.
- **Glass** — `surface` and `sunken` are translucent white (7% / 5%). Panels use `sag-glass` (translucent, 1px light edge, 22px radius, blur; add `sag-glass-strong` for a brighter one). Anything that floats over content (header, bottom navigation, buy and total bars) uses `sag-glass-bar` (a darker blurred pill); drawers, sheets and dialogs become a dark blurred sheet automatically.
- **Text** — `ink` `#F6F1EA` (cream), `ink-muted` `#B9AFA6`, `ink-faint` `#9A9088`. `ink` is LIGHT in this theme: `bg-ink` and `border-ink` are light too.
- **Gold** `#E0BB7C` — `brand` / `sale` / `accent` / `highlight` / `ok`: the primary button fill, links, eyebrows, "% off", "You save", selected edges, progress, ticks. `star` / `sun` `#D2A961` for stars. A gold (or cream) fill ALWAYS carries dark text: `text-[color:var(--sag-on-gold)]`; never `text-white` / `text-ink` on `bg-brand`, `bg-ink` or `bg-sun`.
- **Lines** — `line` translucent white 14%; `control` 32%.
- **Outside these, only where meaning needs it**: `danger` `#FF8A80` for errors (with an icon and words), the green inside the WhatsApp glyph, and the product photos. A chip that sits on a photo (the heart, "1 / N") is a light translucent circle or pill with dark text.
- **Negative constraint**: no opaque white or black blocks, no `bg-white`, no `text-black`, no second accent colour; gradients only in the backdrop lights.
- **Fallbacks** (already in `src/index.css`): with "reduce transparency" or without backdrop blur, panels turn into solid dark surfaces.
- **Print** (the invoice) stays black on white.

Helper classes live in `src/index.css`: `sag-glass`, `sag-glass-strong`, `sag-glass-bar`, `sag-btn-primary` (gold pill, dark text), `sag-btn-outline` (glass pill), `sag-link` (gold), `sag-input` (glass field), `sag-label`, `sag-eyebrow`, `sag-chip` (glass pill; selected = gold with dark text), `sag-badge`, `sag-card` (glass box), `sag-row`, `sag-bar`, `sag-num`, `sag-scroll-x`. The four trust facts are worded once in `src/components/Assurance.tsx`; reuse that component instead of writing new claims.

## 💎 2. Visual Language
**Read this first**: the bullets below describe each part's LAYOUT, and several were written for the earlier white look. Wherever one says "white", "hairline box", "square", "black button" or "tinted", read: a glass panel, round corners, the gold pill, a brighter glass panel. The layouts (the header with the mark and always-open search, the magazine hero, the two-part deal card, two-step checkout and so on) are unchanged.

- **Soft and round**: panels 22-28px radius, photos 14-22px, buttons and chips are pills, icon buttons are round glass circles. Sections sit on the backdrop with a serif title; featured pieces (festive offer, hero, deal, review, facts, product cards, forms, summaries) are glass panels.
- **Typography**: two families only. **Figtree** for all text and every number, at regular and medium weights (semibold for prices, totals and buttons). **Fraunces** (`font-display`, weight 500) for the wordmark, page titles, section titles, the hero title and dialog titles; never for prices, product names, order numbers, forms, buttons or navigation. Eyebrows and a few small labels are letter-spaced capitals in gold; everything else is sentence case. Prices use tabular figures (`sag-num`). Body ≥ 13px, labels ≥ 10.5px, text in fields 16px.
- **Buttons**: primary is a gold pill with dark text ("Buy now", "Checkout", pay, "Shop now"); secondary is a glass pill with light text. Links are gold text. Tap targets at least 44×44px from tablet width up (36-40px on phones, see Phone scale).
- **Icons**: thin outline icons in the text colour or gold; icon buttons are round glass circles.
- **Floating bars**: on phones the header pill, the bottom navigation (current tab = gold pill with dark text) and the product page's buy bar float with a small margin from the screen edges. At most one fixed bottom bar per screen.
- **Motion** (owner, 2026-10-08: calm, one-time, premium, on every page): the new page rises in; every section rises in once as it comes into view (`src/utils/reveal.ts`), with a thin champagne rule drawing under its title; buttons fill from the left on hover and press slightly; product photos zoom slowly under the pointer; the heart beats once, the bag icon bumps once, changed totals settle in. The hero photo drifts slowly, its words rise in, slides change by themselves every 6 seconds with a thin progress line. The festive panel carries a live countdown to the coupon's real last day and a slowly drifting real photo; the deal's price counts once to the real price and shows a clock only for a real end time. No spinning rings, bouncing stickers, waving letters, blinking or marquees. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes all motion.
- **Header** (owner's pick 2-3, 2026-10-08): one white sticky bar: the round logo mark and the wordmark "SUIT AURA GIRLS" (Fraunces capitals) with the small line "Ethnic wear · Delivery across India" at the left, wishlist and bag (real counts) at the right. Under it, on phones and tablets: a square menu button and the always-open search field, then the announcement as a tinted line (`bg-sunken`, champagne text) that rotates the real messages (the live festive round, the online payment percent, cash on delivery when on). The search row and the announcement are part of the page, not of the fixed header (owner, 2026-10-09: the sliding header made the page jump): they scroll away with the page and never come back mid-page; once they are out of sight the header pill shows a menu and a search button instead (on narrow phones in place of the heart). The fixed header must never change height while scrolling, and the search panel opens over the page without pushing it. Desktop: the mark and wordmark, a square hairline search field and the menu row in small capitals.
- **Search**: an underlined field; with an empty field "Trending now" (real product photos with prices) and "Collections" as plain rows with real counts.
- **Phone menu** (owner's pick 2 of the "sleek sidebar" previews, 2026-10-08): a floating dark glass sidebar with a margin from the screen edges: the logo mark, the name and close; a search pill; then groups that fold (Shop, Collections, Your account, Help), each line an icon, its name and a real count in a small badge; the page the shopper is on is a gold pill with dark text; at the bottom the shopper's row (Sign in, or their name and "My account") (`MobileMenuDrawer.tsx`).
- **Bottom navigation (phones)** (owner's pick 2 of the "morphing blob" previews, 2026-10-08, "rising bump"): a floating dark frosted bar a little above the bottom edge whose outline is one drawn shape: it swells up around the current tab and a gold bubble with that tab's icon sits in the swell; on a tab change the swell and the bubble glide across together (`MobileAppNavbar.tsx`, `sag-blobnav*` in `src/index.css`). Home, Shop, Wishlist, Bag (real count), Account, names always shown, the current one in gold. On a page that is none of the five the bar is flat. At most one fixed bottom bar per screen.
- **Hero** (owner's pick 4-2, 2026-10-08, "magazine"): per slide a collage: a narrow vertical caption in small champagne capitals (the owner's badge text, else "New season"), the main photo large and a second real photo of the same style (else the next slide's photo) overlapping its lower right corner in a white frame; under it, left-aligned, the serif title, the real price and "% off", a black "Shop now" and an underlined "Explore". Slides change by themselves every 6 seconds with a thin progress line and "01 / 05". Nothing is written on a photo. Desktop: collage left, words right.
- **Trust facts**: a plain grid of thin champagne icon + the fact between hairlines (two columns on phones, four on desktop). "How your order works" is a hairline box with three numbered rows.
- **Shop by category**: a swipe row of rectangular 3:4 photos with the name under each.
- **Product card**: no card chrome. The photo (4:5, square) with the heart in a small white circle; under it a real badge as a tiny champagne label, the name in two lines, a small rating line with one champagne star when real, the price line, and "Add to bag" as a quiet underlined control. Grids are two columns on phones (12px / 18px gaps); rails give each card about 150px.
- **Homepage sections**: serif title left, underlined "View all" right. Deal of the day (owner's pick 3-3, 2026-10-08) is one ink-bordered card in two parts: a dark left part with "DEAL OF THE DAY", the real discount percent very large in champagne serif and "OFF" (plus the clock only for a real end time), and a white right part with a small photo, the name, the price, "You save ₹X" and a black "View deal". Customer reviews is the one tinted section: a centred serif italic quote with small champagne stars, the name and "Verified purchase" when true, and the real average and count.
- **Shop page**: serif title with the real count, square outline quick filters, one Filter | Sort row in small capitals between hairlines (sticky under the header), the grid.
- **Product page**: one large square-cornered photo with a "1 / N" count and a row of small thumbnails; the badge as an eyebrow, the name, heart and share as plain icons, the price, the rating line, the offers as one tinted box of plain lines, round colour swatches, square size boxes, black "Buy now" over outline "Add to bag", the four facts between hairlines, the PIN code check as a hairline area, then details as "+" rows. The fixed buy bar is white with a top hairline.
- **Bag**: serif title, a tinted savings notice, lines separated by hairlines, a hairline coupon box, plain price rows and a black "Checkout".
- **Checkout** (two steps: address, then payment; one "Full name" field): a white bar "SECURE CHECKOUT" with a thin lock and an underlined "Help", the steps as small capitals, hairline boxes for the order and the address, the title "How would you like to pay?". Email stays required. **Built to win prepaid orders** (owner's pick from the checkout preview, 2026-10-09): the address step asks in the order mobile, PIN code and city (filled from the PIN), state, full name, house, street, email, and shows the applied coupon above the button; the best coupon is applied by itself once (only coupons without a per-customer limit; removing it stops that for the visit). On the payment step "Pay online now" is one large gold card, chosen from the start, with the amount, the cash-on-delivery total struck through and the true perks; cash on delivery is a small row under it with its total and "₹X more"; a thin bar compares both totals. On phones a "Pay with" row (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, other) puts the tapped app first in the Razorpay window (`UPI_APPS`, `razorpayDisplayConfig` in `src/lib/payments.ts`); nothing is preselected. Picking cash on delivery asks once per visit ("Pay online and keep ₹X", with "Keep cash on delivery" one tap away). A payment that fails in the window shows a panel with the same order number and "Try again" (the same attempt key, never a second order) and a WhatsApp line. Under the pay button: the gateway line, "Your UPI PIN is entered only in your own UPI app", and the festive coupon's real time left when that coupon is on the order. **Cash on delivery** (Admin → Settings switches it and sets the charge): two hairline options, "Pay online now" (the online payment discount) and "Cash on delivery" (the charge is paid by UPI through Razorpay to confirm, the rest is collected in cash at delivery; no online discount); with cash on delivery chosen a tinted box shows the prepaid saving and a way back to online payment. The order stores `payMode`, `codAdvance` and `codDue`; order pages, emails and Admin always show what was paid online and what is still to be collected. PayU is never used for the advance.
- **Order placed**: centred on white: an outline circle with a tick, the eyebrow, the serif title, the real order number, a hairline box with the items and payment rows, black "Track order". **Order tracking**: a hairline box with the state in words and a thin four-part progress line, the reached stages as a plain timeline with their real times, then "Courier tracking" (only with a real link) and "Help".
- **Sign in**: a white page with the logo mark, serif "Welcome", outline "Continue with Google", the email field and a black "Send code". **My account**: the latest order as a hairline box, then plain rows with thin champagne icons.
- **Wishlist**: a two-column photo grid with name, price and an outline "Move to bag". There is no per-style price-drop switch (the store has no such setting).
- **Help and policy pages**: an eyebrow, the serif title, a tinted "In short" box whose points are taken from that policy's own text, then the full text in numbered hairline sections.
- **Footer**: a glass panel: wordmark, links in columns, plain social icons, the address and one line of small print from the store's real facts.
- **Festive month**: two rounds, Navratri (code NAVRATRI, till 20 Oct; it replaced Dussehra on 2026-10-08 at the owner's request) and Diwali (code DIWALI, 21 Oct to 8 Nov). The homepage panel is dark with a thin champagne double frame: a real photo of diyas being lit (`public/festive/`, CC0; the owner rejected the drawn "cartoon" scene, so festive imagery is always a real photo or classical painting that is free to use, never a drawing), the offer, a live countdown to the coupon's real last day and the two buttons. The shop page carries a slim dark row. All of it is drawn only while that round's coupon is live (`FestiveBanner.tsx`).
- **Phone scale**: on phones (below 640px) the layout sits one size smaller: `src/index.css` sets the root size to 90.6%, body text 14px, buttons and fields 46px high; text inside fields stays 16px so a phone does not zoom on focus. Do not size phone elements up again.
- **Focus**: a visible 2px gold outline on everything (a light one on a gold fill).

## 📸 3. Model & Media Treatment
- **Clear catalog images**: never cover product photos with gradients, text or badges. The one thing allowed on a product card photo is the wishlist heart in a white circle in the top-right corner (the brand watermark sits top-left). Badges and prices sit below the image.
- **No watermark overlaps**: catalog photos already carry the brand watermark; never overlay logos on them.
- Portrait photos use `aspect-[4/5]` with `object-cover object-top`.
- `public/cropped_circle_image.png` is another store's logo and must never be shown; the round mark in the header is `public/suit-aura-mark-132.webp`, cut from this store's own logo.

- **Where photos and reels live** (owner, 2026-10-07: moved off Cloudinary, which is gone from this project and must not come back): the Cloudflare R2 bucket `suitauragirls-media`, read straight from `https://media.suitauragirls.in` (Cloudflare's CDN; a photo costs no Worker request and no bandwidth fee). Nothing is resized on the fly. Every photo is stored in ready-made files: `i/<folder>/<id>/w160|w320|w480|w640|w828|w1100|w1600.webp` plus `j480.jpg` (emails) and `j1200.jpg` (link previews, the AI photo reader); a reel is `v/<folder>/<id>/play.mp4` and `poster.jpg`. The address saved in the database is `…/w1100.webp` or `…/play.mp4`.
  - Show a photo only through `getCleanImageUrl(url, width)` (`src/utils/imageHelper.ts`), which picks the stored size that fits; never write a size into a URL by hand. Links saved before the move are translated by `toMediaUrl` in `src/lib/media.ts`.
  - Uploads: the browser makes every size first (`makeImageFiles` in `src/lib/media.ts`), then sends each file to `PUT /api/media` with a grant the server signed for that one photo (`createMediaGrant` in `api/_lib/media.js`; admin via `/api/admin-config?action=media-grant`, customers via the review and return forms). A 5 MB phone photo goes up as about 0.3-1 MB in all. Reel videos are stored as uploaded: MP4 only, 50 MB at most.
  - Deleting a photo deletes every stored size of it (`deleteMedia`).
  - Admin → Usage & limits shows the bucket's storage and requests against R2's free amounts (10 GB, 1 million writes and 10 million reads a month) and an estimate of how many visits the free plan can carry, from measured numbers only.

## 🧭 4b. Admin panel (redesigned 2026-10-08)
The owner is not technical: every admin page uses plain words and the same skeleton (serif title with one line saying what the page is for, 2-4 real key numbers in small glass tiles, filters in one row, the main list or form in titled glass panels, one gold primary action, clear empty states). Shared pieces live in `src/components/admin/adminUi.tsx`, `kidsToysShared.tsx`, `PaymentBadge.tsx` and `AdminPaymentGateways.tsx`.
- **Dashboard**: "Right now" (people on the site this moment, visitors today, orders and sales today), "Today's to-do", cash to collect on cash-on-delivery orders, live visitors with their page trail, "What customers are doing", visitors by hour, Today / This week / This month, the path to an order (funnel), the last 30 days, "Needs your attention", top products, sources and devices. **Reports** is the day / week / month history with CSV.
- **Live visitors** come from the `presence` table (one row per visitor: city from Cloudflare, device, source, current page, the last 12 pages; never the IP address). The storefront sends a small signal on each page change and every 2 minutes (`src/utils/presence.ts`, `api/_lib/presence.js`, event `presence` on `/api/track`); the admin reads `GET /api/admin-config?action=live` every 15 seconds while the dashboard is open. Presence has its own daily write budget (`PRESENCE_WRITE_BUDGET`): when it is used up only the live view pauses for the day; visit counting and orders are never affected.
- Top bar: search everything (`/` or Ctrl+K), quick actions, an optional sound for new paid orders. Order details carry a printable packing slip and ready-made WhatsApp messages.
- Every number in the admin is real; a day without data says "No data".

## ✅ 5. Honesty Rules
- Never show fake coupons, fake ratings/reviews, invented delivery dates, countdowns that reset, stock numbers that are not real, or service claims the owner has not confirmed.
- Every price, discount, coupon and total shown in the browser is only a preview; the server (`api/orders.js`) recalculates everything before payment.
